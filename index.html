Paper Roll

The roll is split into four separate sections: control holes (registers 1–7 and 15, percussion 8–14, division markers 16–18), then the Bass, Accompaniment and Melody pipe ranges, each with its own columns so the same MIDI pitch in two divisions no longer lands on the same hole. The roll runs top-to-bottom and is mirrored horizontally, so the sections read right-to-left on the image.
