Generated Organ MIDI

Exactly 10 organ output tracks. Cello receives Bass; Forte receives Bass + Melody; Violin/Flute/Flageolet/Bourdon/Glockenspiel receive Melody. Input velocity is ignored and output notes use a fixed velocity.

101-Hole Paper Roll

Holes 1–14 are controls/percussion. Bass = 15–32, Accompaniment = 33–64, Melody = 65–101. Roll runs top-to-bottom and is mirrored horizontally.
